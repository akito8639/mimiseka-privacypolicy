Stories Left at a Place — Terms of Use

Effective: September 4, 2026 (version 1)

These terms cover the "Leave a Story at This Place" feature of MimiSeka Scia (the "App"). They set out the agreement between the MimiSeka Project ("we", "us") and you when you use this feature.

There are three ways to take part:

  • Placing — leaving a story born from a photo you took, at the place where you took it
  • Receiving — listening to a story someone else left, when you stand at that place
  • Reading provided works — listening to stories or descriptions that an author, publisher, municipality, museum, or similar provider has registered for that place

These terms apply in addition to the App's license terms (the App Store standard EULA) and our Privacy Policy. The first time you place a story, and the first time you open a story someone else left, the App shows the key points of these terms and asks for your agreement. From that moment you use the feature under these terms.

The Japanese version at terms.html is the governing text. This English translation is provided for convenience; if the two differ, the Japanese version prevails.

In short

  • A story you place reaches people standing at that place for one year, without your name. You can stop placing it at any time.
  • You keep the copyright. We may narrate the story as audio, translate it, and compile it with others into a record of that place. This license continues after you stop placing the story and after it expires.
  • Offensive, sexual, or violent content, other people's personal information, other people's works, and advertising may not be placed. A reported story stops being delivered immediately.
  • Stories left by others and provided works are for listening on the spot. They may not be saved, recorded, or reposted.

1. Definitions

  • The Feature: "Leave a Story at This Place" in the App, including placing, receiving, and reading provided works.
  • Placed story: the text of a story you placed through the Feature, its narration audio, the capture date, and the capture location.
  • Story someone left: a story placed by another user. The App shows these under this name.
  • Provided work: a story or description registered for a place by a provider — an author, publisher, municipality, museum, shrine, temple, facility, or similar — under an arrangement with us.
  • Receiving: standing at the place and listening to (or reading) a story someone left or a provided work in the App.

2. Placing a Story

2.1 What you can place

You may place only stories created in the App from photos you took yourself (including stories you edited). You must hold the rights needed to grant us the license in these terms. Stories created from someone else's photo, or containing someone else's writing, may not be placed.

2.2 No name is shown

A placed story carries no name or display name. It reaches recipients as a "story someone left", together with the month and year of capture. However, anything you write in the story text — including your own or another person's name, address, or contact details — is delivered as is. Do not write personally identifiable information in the text.

2.3 Copyright and the license you grant us

You keep the copyright in your placed story. We do not take ownership of it.

You grant us a free, worldwide, non-exclusive license to use the placed story for the following purposes:

  1. Delivering it to users standing at that place, through the App and our websites (including displaying the text and playing the narration audio)
  2. Synthesizing and re-synthesizing narration audio; translating it; creating summaries and captions; adjusting punctuation and line breaks for narration
  3. Collecting the stories placed at that place and editing, excerpting, and compiling them into a record of the place (an e-book, printed matter, an audio work, a web page, and the like), and publishing and distributing that record
  4. Providing the stories placed at a place to that place's provider (the provider of the provided work, or the owner or manager of the place), and creating the record in 3 together with them
  5. Processing for content review (including automated checks), handling reports, preventing abuse, and improving the Feature

Within these purposes, we may sublicense to providers and to businesses we engage for production.

Because placed stories are handled anonymously, your name is not shown in the record described in 3 either. You agree not to assert moral rights (the right of attribution and the right of integrity) against us or our sublicensees. We will not, however, alter a story in a way that distorts its meaning.

This license continues after you stop placing the story (2.4) and after the story expires (2.5). Because it is practically impossible to ask everyone for a new license later, please review this point before placing.

2.4 Stopping

You can stop placing a story at any time from the App. Once stopped, the story is no longer delivered to anyone new. However:

  • Temporary copies on the devices of people who already received it cannot be removed by us (received stories are designed not to be saveable, so these copies normally disappear after a while)
  • The original remains on our servers, and the license in 2.3 continues. A story cannot be removed afterwards from a record that has already been created under 2.3(3)

If you would like a story excluded from record-making, contact us through Section 8. For stories not yet incorporated into a record, we will accommodate this as far as we can.

Stopping is possible only from the device you used to place the story (or devices synced with the same Apple Account). Deleting the App may make it impossible to stop. Please review your placed stories before deleting the App.

2.5 How long a placed story lasts

A placed story is delivered for one year from the day it was placed, after which it stops automatically. Placing it again starts a new year from that day. After expiry the original remains with us and the license in 2.3 continues.

2.6 What may not be placed

The Feature has a zero-tolerance policy toward objectionable content and abusive use. The following may not be placed. If placed, we may stop delivery, remove the story, and prevent you from placing or receiving in the future, without notice.

  • Offensive content: defamation, discrimination, harassment, or threats against any person or group
  • Sexual content, or sexualized commentary about a person
  • Content that encourages violence, self-harm, or dangerous acts
  • Personally identifiable information about others: names, addresses, contact details, detailed descriptions of appearance or movements
  • Advertising, solicitation, links to external sites, email addresses, or repeated identical content
  • Works or trademarks of others used without their permission
  • Impersonation, or content that misleads people with false claims
  • Content that troubles the place's owner, manager, or people nearby, or that is based on photographing in restricted areas
  • Content that violates laws or public order, or that we otherwise judge inappropriate

2.7 Automated check before placing

When you place a story, its text goes through an automated content check before publication. A story that fails the check is neither stored nor published. We do not tell you what triggered the check. If the check itself cannot be performed, the story cannot be placed. Even a story that passed the automated check may later be stopped if we judge it to fall under 2.6.

2.8 Reports and what happens next

Recipients can report a story someone left. A reported story stops being delivered immediately, before we review it. We then review the content and decide whether to restore or remove it. We do not notify the author of the outcome or the reasons.

We may refuse further placements from, and stop delivering stories to, anyone who placed content under 2.6 or who is reported repeatedly. In cases of serious violation or suspected illegal activity, we may provide information to the police or other public authorities.

2.9 Age

The Feature is for people aged 13 and over. If you are under 18, obtain a parent's or guardian's consent before placing a story. Placed stories are seen by other people.

2.10 No guarantees

We do not guarantee that a placed story will reach any particular person or any particular number of times, that it will be stored permanently, or that the place will remain part of the Feature. We may remove a place from the Feature when our arrangement with its provider ends or for operational reasons, in which case stories placed there stop being delivered.

3. Receiving a Story Someone Left

3.1 For listening on the spot

A story someone left is meant to be heard (or read) while standing at the place. The App is designed so that it cannot be saved. Do not capture the screen, record it, transcribe it, repost it on social media or the web, or distribute it to others. Use beyond quotation permitted by copyright law may infringe the author's rights.

Authors are anonymous. Do not attempt to guess or identify who the author is from the text, the capture month, or anything else.

3.2 Responsibility for content

A story someone left is that person's writing. Although we run an automated check before placement, we do not guarantee that the content is accurate, lawful, or suitable for you. What a story says is not necessarily a fact about the place.

If you encounter a story that is offensive or appears to fall under 2.6, report it from that story's screen. A reported story stops being delivered immediately. You can also block the author so you no longer receive their stories.

3.3 Using reports properly

Reports are for content under 2.6. Do not report a story merely because you dislike it, report the same story repeatedly, or report many stories at once. If we judge that reports are being abused, we may stop counting reports from that person.

3.4 Blocking

Blocking works per author and is recorded on our servers. We do not tell you who the blocked author is, nor tell the author that they were blocked. To lift a block, contact us through Section 8.
